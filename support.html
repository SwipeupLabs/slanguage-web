Using Slanguage

Slanguage helps you turn stiff, textbook-style expressions into natural, native-sounding speech. To get started, simply enter the text you want to translate, choose your target language, pick a tone (such as Standard, Gen Z, Social, Business, or Romantic), and tap translate.

Slanguage will return a translated result tailored to the tone you selected, along with helpful context and usage notes where available.

💳

Subscription & Billing

Subscriptions purchased on iOS are managed directly through your Apple App Store account and are billed by Apple, not by SwipeUp Labs.

To view, change, or cancel your Slanguage subscription on iOS, open the App Store app on your device, tap your profile picture, then tap Subscriptions and follow the on-screen instructions.

SwipeUp Labs is unable to directly cancel Apple App Store subscriptions on your behalf, as subscription management is handled exclusively by Apple for purchases made through the App Store.

⚠️

Translation Issues

If you receive an inaccurate, unexpected, or unhelpful translation, you can report it directly through the in-app reporting feature. Tap the report button that appears on the translation result screen and briefly describe the issue. Your feedback helps us improve Slanguage translations for everyone.
